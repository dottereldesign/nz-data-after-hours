What counts in the ranking?

The dollar gap is player losses minus gross single-district approvals. The percentage is those approvals divided by losses. Shared and national grants, refunds and societies’ own spending sit outside this comparison. Recipients are grouped by their recorded organisation name. Spelling variants can appear separately.

How the figures work

Player losses are gaming machine profit at pubs and clubs, summed across four quarters in nominal NZ dollars. The grant bar counts gross approvals recorded against one district in the same calendar year. Refunds are shown in the records. Shared, national and unknown-area awards stay outside that bar. These figures describe where play and grants were recorded. They cannot trace a player’s money to a particular club.
