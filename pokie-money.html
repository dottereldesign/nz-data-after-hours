What counts in the ranking?

Gap = money lost minus grants approved. The percentage shows grants per $100 lost. We count grants listed for one district, even when the form leaves the benefit area blank. Others may benefit too. We leave out shared grants, national grants, refunds and societies’ own spending. Different spellings of a club’s name can appear separately.

How much was lost? How much went to grants?

How the figures work

Losses are bets minus prizes at local pubs and clubs over the year. Grants are amounts approved for one recorded district, before refunds. You can see refunds in the records. Shared, national and unknown-area grants are left out.
